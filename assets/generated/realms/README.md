# Realm hero artwork

These nine house plates are original, AI-generated fan-art compositions made
for The Raven Wall. They are not official HBO imagery and do not reproduce
promotional stills. The artwork is used as a consistent visual surface while
the matching bottom constellation rail and outlined call-to-action are part of
every plate. The rail and CTA are also live HTML hotspots on the home route,
so the wallpaper never sacrifices keyboard access or navigation.

The `*-v2.png` files are the current master plates. They share a locked
1672x941 composition, nine-house rail order, arrow endpoints, and CTA bounds.
